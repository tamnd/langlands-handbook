# Modular forms as functions on adele groups

Let $\Delta(\tau) = \sum_{n \ge 1} \tau(n) q^n$ be Ramanujan's cusp form of weight 12. Volume 4 treats $\Delta$ as a function on the upper half plane $\mathbb{H}$ with the transformation law $\Delta(\gamma\tau) = (c\tau + d)^{12} \Delta(\tau)$ for $\gamma \in SL_2(\mathbb{Z})$. The Hecke operator $T_p$ acts on it by a sum over $p + 1$ matrices, and $T_p \Delta = \tau(p) \Delta$. This description treats the prime $p$ through an operator, while the archimedean place is the space $\mathbb{H}$ itself. The adelic description puts all the places on the same footing. It replaces $\Delta$ by a function $\phi_\Delta$ on the group $GL_2(\mathbb{A})$, where $\mathbb{A}$ is the ring of adeles of $\mathbb{Q}$:
$$\phi_\Delta(\gamma g_\infty k) = \det(g_\infty)^{6} \, j(g_\infty, i)^{-12} \, \Delta(g_\infty i), \qquad \gamma \in GL_2(\mathbb{Q}), \ g_\infty \in GL_2^+(\mathbb{R}), \ k \in GL_2(\hat{\mathbb{Z}}).$$
Each element of $GL_2(\mathbb{A})$ has such a factorization, and the value does not depend on the factorization. The function $\phi_\Delta$ is invariant under $GL_2(\mathbb{Q})$ on the left and under $GL_2(\hat{\mathbb{Z}})$ on the right. The operator $T_p$ becomes the average of the right translates of $\phi_\Delta$ by the double coset $GL_2(\mathbb{Z}_p)\,\mathrm{diag}(p, 1)\,GL_2(\mathbb{Z}_p)$ of the group $GL_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. It acts by the scalar $p^{-5}\tau(p)$. The holomorphy of $\Delta$ becomes a differential equation for the action of the Lie algebra of $GL_2(\mathbb{R})$. The weight 12 becomes a character of the rotation group $SO(2)$.

This chapter proves these statements for every modular form of weight $k$, level $N$ and nebentypus $\chi$. The result is a dictionary between the classical and the adelic languages. Chapter 2 starts from the adelic side and defines automorphic forms on $GL_2(\mathbb{A})$ in general. The dictionary of this chapter shows that the classical forms of Volume 4 are a special case.

Section 1.1 defines the group $GL_2(\mathbb{A})$, the compact open subgroups $K_0(N)$, $K_1(N)$ and $K(N)$ of $GL_2(\mathbb{A}_f)$ that correspond to the congruence subgroups $\Gamma_0(N)$, $\Gamma_1(N)$ and $\Gamma(N)$, and the Iwasawa decomposition. Section 1.2 proves strong approximation for $SL_2$ and deduces the decomposition $GL_2(\mathbb{A}) = GL_2(\mathbb{Q}) GL_2^+(\mathbb{R}) K_0(N)$. It identifies the double coset space $GL_2(\mathbb{Q}) \backslash GL_2(\mathbb{A}) / K_0(N)$ with $\Gamma_0(N) \backslash GL_2^+(\mathbb{R})$. Section 1.3 attaches to a modular form $f$ the function $\phi_f$ and proves its transformation laws. Section 1.4 proves the analytic properties of $\phi_f$: it is annihilated by the lowering operator, it is an eigenfunction of the Casimir operator with eigenvalue $\frac{k}{2}(1 - \frac{k}{2})$, it has moderate growth, and it is cuspidal if and only if $f$ is a cusp form. Section 1.5 proves the converse: each function on $GL_2(\mathbb{A})$ with these properties is $\phi_f$ for a unique $f$. Section 1.6 defines the local Hecke algebras, identifies $T_p$ with the convolution by the characteristic function of $K_p\,\mathrm{diag}(p, 1)\,K_p$, and states the full dictionary as Theorem 1.6.12.
