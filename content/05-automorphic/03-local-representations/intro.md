# Local representations of GL(2)

Three modular forms show what this chapter is about. The first is the discriminant $\Delta = \sum \tau(n) q^n$, of weight 12 and level 1. The second is the newform of weight 2 and level 11 of the elliptic curve $y^2 + y = x^3 - x^2 - 10x - 20$, with $q$-expansion $q - 2q^2 - q^3 + 2q^4 + q^5 + \dots$. The third is the newform of weight 2 and level 27 of an elliptic curve of conductor 27. Chapter 4 attaches to each of them an automorphic representation $\pi = \bigotimes_v \pi_v$ of $GL_2(\mathbb{A})$, and its local component $\pi_p$ at a prime $p$ is an irreducible admissible representation of $GL_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. These three examples give three different kinds of local components. For $\Delta$ and every $p$, the representation $\pi_p$ has a vector fixed by $GL_2(\mathbb{Z}_p)$. It is an unramified principal series, and it is determined by two complex numbers whose sum is $\tau(p) p^{-11/2}$. For the curve of level 11 and $p = 11$, the representation $\pi_{11}$ is the Steinberg representation, the representation of $GL_2(\mathbb{Q}_{11})$ on the locally constant functions on $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q}_{11})$ modulo the constants. The coefficient $a_{11} = 1$ is its eigenvalue under a local Hecke operator. For the curve of conductor 27 and $p = 3$, the representation $\pi_3$ is supercuspidal: it is not a constituent of any induced representation, and its matrix coefficients have compact support modulo the center. We prove this from its conductor alone in Section 3.6.

The chapter describes the irreducible admissible representations of $GL_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ with the tools that the global theory needs. Section 3.1 treats the normalized principal series and its Jacquet module. Section 3.2 gives the irreducibility criterion and the special representations. Section 3.3 treats supercuspidal representations and proves the classification. Section 3.4 proves the Satake isomorphism for $GL_2$ and relates the local Hecke eigenvalue to $a_p$. Section 3.5 treats the Kirillov model, the spherical Whittaker function and the local $L$-factors. Section 3.6 is Casselman's theory of the conductor and of newforms, the local form of the theory of Atkin and Lehner of Volume 4.

Volume 6, Chapter 1 is the general theory of smooth representations of $GL_n(K)$. It proves the reducibility criterion and the classification for $GL_2(K)$ in full, and we do not repeat these proofs. We cite the exact place in Volume 6, and we prove here what Volume 6 does not contain: the explicit Satake isomorphism of $GL_2$, the spherical Whittaker function, the fixed vectors under the congruence subgroups, and Casselman's newforms with the local $U_p$ eigenvalues. Everything holds for an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field $K$. For the global theory over $\mathbb{Q}$, take $K = \mathbb{Q}_p$, $\varpi = p$ and $q = p$.

We follow Bump, *Automorphic forms and representations* (Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 55, 1997), Chapter 4. The handbook cites it as [Bum97]. For the local theory we also use [BH06], Chapters 2 to 4, the book of Jacquet and Langlands [JL70], Chapter 1, and for the theory of newforms the paper of Casselman, "On some results of Atkin and Lehner" (Mathematische Annalen 201, 1973). The volume also cites Gelbart, *Automorphic forms on adele groups* (1975), as [Gel75], and Getz and Hahn, *An introduction to automorphic representations* (2024), as [GH24]. The normalizations are those of Volume 0, Section 3.2. In particular the Satake parameters are unitary and the additive character of $\mathbb{Q}_p$ is the standard character $\psi_p$, which is trivial on $\mathbb{Z}_p$ and not on $p^{-1}\mathbb{Z}_p$.

The Lean project proves a large part of Sections 3.1 to 3.3 for an arbitrary nonarchimedean local field. It has the principal series, its Jacquet module, the irreducibility criterion, the Steinberg representations and the classification of the irreducible admissible representations that are not supercuspidal. The line `Lean: ...` under a statement names the Lean declarations. The Lean project has no spherical Hecke algebra of $GL_2(K)$, no Kirillov model and no newforms, so the statements of Sections 3.4 to 3.6 have the line `Lean: not stated yet`.
