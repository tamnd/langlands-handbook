## Exercises

**Exercise 1.** Let $A$ and $B$ be complex algebras with $B$ commutative, and let $M$ be a finite-dimensional irreducible $A \otimes B$-module. Prove that $B$ acts on $M$ by a character and that $M$ is irreducible over $A$.

**Exercise 2.** For $f \in S_k(SL_2(\mathbb{Z}))$ let $\phi_f(\gamma g_\infty k_f) = (f|_kg_\infty)(i)$ for $\gamma \in GL_2(\mathbb{Q})$, $g_\infty \in GL_2^+(\mathbb{R})$ and $k_f \in GL_2(\hat{\mathbb{Z}})$. Prove that $\pi(T_p)\phi_f = p^{1 - k/2}\phi_{T_pf}$, with $\pi(T_p)$ built from the cosets of Lemma 4.2.5.

**Exercise 3.** Let $\chi_1, \chi_2$ be unramified characters of $\mathbb{Q}_p^\times$. Prove that $U_p = $ the characteristic function of $K_0(p)a(p)K_0(p)$ acts on the two-dimensional space $(\chi_1 \times \chi_2)^{K_0(p)}$ with eigenvalues $p^{1/2}\chi_1(p)$ and $p^{1/2}\chi_2(p)$. Deduce that $U_p$ acts by $\mu(p)$ on the newvector of $\mu\mathrm{St}$.

**Exercise 4.** Let $\chi = \chi_{-4}$ and let $f$ be a newform of level $N$ with $4 \nmid N$. Prove that $\pi_f$ and $\pi_f \otimes (\chi \circ \det)$ have isomorphic components at all $p \equiv 1 \bmod 4$. For $f = f_{11}$, prove that they are not isomorphic, by looking at $p = 3$.

**Exercise 5.** In $\mathcal{H}(G_p, K_p)$ prove that $T_p * T_p = T_{p^2} + (p + 1)R_p$, where $T_{p^2}$ is the characteristic function of $K_pa(p^2)K_p$.

**Exercise 6.** Find two distinct characters of $\mathbb{Q}^\times \backslash \mathbb{A}^\times$ that agree at infinitely many places. Which step of the proof of Lemma 4.4.3 fails for an infinite set $S$?

**Exercise 7.** Let $\pi$ be cuspidal and $t \in \mathbb{C}$. Prove that $\pi \otimes |\det|^t$ is automorphic, that $L(s, \pi \otimes |\det|^t) = L(s + t, \pi)$ and that $\varepsilon(s, \pi \otimes |\det|^t) = \varepsilon(s + t, \pi)$.

**Exercise 8.** Let $k \ge 4$ be even and $L(s) = \zeta(s)\zeta(s - k + 1)$. Prove that $\Lambda(s) = (2\pi)^{-s}\Gamma(s)L(s)$ satisfies $\Lambda(k - s) = (-1)^{k/2}\Lambda(s)$, find its poles, and follow the proof of Theorem 4.6.1 to recover the modularity of the Eisenstein series of weight $k$.

**Exercise 9.** Let $f$ be a newform of weight 2 with rational coefficients and $p \nmid N$. Prove that $\alpha_p$ is a root of unity if and only if $a_p = 0$, or $p = 2$ and $a_2 = \pm 2$, or $p = 3$ and $a_3 = \pm 3$. Apply this to $f_{11}$ at $p = 2$.

**Exercise 10.** Let $\pi$ be cuspidal and $\eta$ a character of $\mathbb{Q}^\times \backslash \mathbb{A}^\times$. Prove that $\pi \otimes (\eta \circ \det)$ is cuspidal, find its central character and its components, and prove $L(s, \pi_p \otimes \eta_p) = (1 - \alpha_p\eta_p(p)p^{-s})^{-1}(1 - \beta_p\eta_p(p)p^{-s})^{-1}$ when $\pi_p$ and $\eta_p$ are unramified.

## Notes and history

Hecke found the converse theorem in level one in 1936. Weil added the twists in 1967. Jacquet and Langlands [JL70] gave the adelic theory of this chapter, and Flath proved the tensor product theorem in 1979. Casselman proved the theory of local newforms in 1973. Deligne proved the Ramanujan conjecture for weight $k \ge 2$ [Del74], and Deligne and Serre proved it for weight 1 [DS74]. The nodes of the chapter are LGAGRC05KF, LGV8B6TVWZ, LG75ERTV5K and LG0S1WN9ND, all in Section 4.7.
