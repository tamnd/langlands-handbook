# The map and the reading list

The handbook is one of four parts of the project tamnd/langlands. The map of the project is a graph of the statements of the program and of their dependencies. The Lean library states and proves the nodes of the map. The handbook explains them to mathematicians. This chapter describes the map, the dependencies between the volumes, and the books that a reader can use next to the handbook.

The first section describes the map: its nodes, its records and the computed status. The second section gives the dependencies between the volumes and the tiers of the map. The third section gives a reading list, with one sentence on each book. The last section has a few exercises and the notes.
