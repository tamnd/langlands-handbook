## A reading list

Each volume of the handbook follows one or two standard texts, and it says which ones. The list below gives these texts, grouped by subject, with one sentence on each. A reader who wants a second account of a topic should start with the first book in its group.

### Surveys

- Gelbart, "An elementary introduction to the Langlands program", Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 10 (1984): the program for $GL_1$ and $GL_2$, explained from class field theory and modular forms, with few prerequisites.
- Arthur, "The principle of functoriality", Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 40 (2003): the statement of functoriality and its relation to the trace formula, by the author of the endoscopic classification.
- Calegari, "Reciprocity in the Langlands program since Fermat's Last Theorem" (2021): a survey of the results on reciprocity after the proof of the modularity of semistable elliptic curves.

### Number fields, local fields and class field theory

- Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Grundlehren 322 (1999): valuations, local and global fields, class field theory in an abstract form, and zeta and $L$-functions, in one volume.
- Serre, *Local Fields*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 67 (1979) [Ser79]: discrete valuations, ramification groups, group cohomology and local class field theory, with complete proofs.
- Cassels and Fröhlich (eds.), *Algebraic Number Theory* (1967, second edition 2010): the proceedings of a conference that contain Serre's local class field theory [Ser67], Tate's global class field theory, Tate's thesis and a set of exercises.
- Milne, *Class Field Theory*, course notes [Mil20]: the Lubin-Tate construction and the cohomological proofs of local and global class field theory; it is the main text of Volume 3.
- Tate, "Number theoretic background" [Tat79]: Weil groups, their representations, and local constants, in the normalizations of this handbook.

### Groups and their representations

- Springer, *Linear Algebraic Groups*, second edition (1998): the structure theory of reductive groups over an algebraically closed field and over an arbitrary field.
- Milne, *Algebraic Groups*, Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 170 (2017): algebraic groups as group schemes over a field, with the classification of the split reductive groups by root data.
- Knapp, *Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction*, second edition (2002): the structure theory of real semisimple Lie groups, with root systems, the Iwasawa and Cartan decompositions and the integration formulas.

### Modular forms and elliptic curves

- Diamond and Shurman, *A First Course in Modular Forms*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 228 (2005): modular curves, Hecke operators, the Eichler-Shimura relation and the several forms of the modularity theorem.
- Silverman, *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 106 (second edition 2009): elliptic curves over finite, local and global fields, and the theorem of Mordell and Weil.
- Shimura, *Introduction to the Arithmetic Theory of Automorphic Functions* (1971): the arithmetic of modular forms and modular curves, with the construction of the abelian variety attached to a newform of weight 2.

### Automorphic representations

- Bump, *Automorphic Forms and Representations*, Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 55 (1997): the passage from modular forms to automorphic representations of $GL_2$, with the local theory over $\mathbb{R}$ and over $p$-adic fields.
- Gelbart, *Automorphic Forms on Adele Groups*, Annals of Mathematics Studies 83 (1975): the adelic theory of $GL_2$ and the theory of Jacquet and Langlands, in a short form.
- Getz and Hahn, *An Introduction to Automorphic Representations*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 300 (2024): algebraic groups, adeles, automorphic representations and trace formulae in one recent textbook.
- Bushnell and Henniart, *The Local Langlands Conjecture for GL(2)*, Grundlehren 335 (2006): a complete local proof of the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_2$ over a nonarchimedean local field.

### Galois representations and modularity

- Harris and Taylor, *The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties*, Annals of Mathematics Studies 151 (2001): the proof of the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_n$ by the cohomology of Shimura varieties.
- Cornell, Silverman and Stevens (eds.), *Modular Forms and Fermat's Last Theorem* (1997): the lectures of a conference on the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem, from the basic theory to the deformation theory.
- Darmon, Diamond and Taylor, "Fermat's Last Theorem" (1995): a detailed account of the proof of Wiles and Taylor-Wiles, with the deformation theory and the Hecke algebras.
- Arthur, *The Endoscopic Classification of Representations: Orthogonal and Symplectic Groups*, Colloquium Publications 61 (2013): the classification of the automorphic representations of the quasi-split orthogonal and symplectic groups in terms of those of $GL_n$.

### The geometric program

- Frenkel, "Lectures on the Langlands program and conformal field theory" (2007): an introduction to the geometric Langlands program, from the function field case to the methods of conformal field theory.

The papers on the map have paper records in tamnd/langlands, with the version that the project read. The handbook cites them by the key of the record, as in [Ser67].
