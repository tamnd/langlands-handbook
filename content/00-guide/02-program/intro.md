# The program in one chapter

This chapter states the main results and the main conjectures of the Langlands program, with the normalizations that the rest of the handbook uses. It gives no proofs. Each statement has a reference to the volume that proves it or that explains its proof.

The first section treats the group $GL_1$, where the program is class field theory. The second section treats $GL_2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, where the main result is the modularity of elliptic curves. The third section states the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_n$, the global conjectures and the geometric program, and it says which parts are theorems and which are open. The last section has exercises and the notes.

The reader needs only the definitions of local fields, of the adeles and of modular forms. Volume 1 and Volume 4 give these definitions. A reader who does not know them can read the statements as a plan of the handbook and come back to this chapter later.
