# How to read this handbook

This handbook explains the Langlands program, from class field theory to the open conjectures. It is for graduate students and for mathematicians who do not read Lean. This chapter says what the handbook contains, who it is for, and how a reader can check each claim.

The first section describes the three kinds of objects of the program and the two sides of the book. The second section lists the volumes, the order in which to read them, and the background that each one needs. The third section explains the numbers of the statements, the lines under them that name the Lean code, and the statuses that these lines show.
