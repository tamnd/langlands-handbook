# Representations of p-adic groups and the local Langlands correspondence

This volume is about the local side of the Langlands program. Let $K$ be a nonarchimedean local field. Volume 3 proved that the characters of $K^\times$ correspond to the characters of the Weil group $W_K$. This is the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_1$. The correspondence for $GL_n$ replaces the characters of $K^\times$ by the irreducible smooth representations of $GL_n(K)$, and it replaces the characters of $W_K$ by the Weil-Deligne representations of dimension $n$. The two sides are very different. On one side there are infinite-dimensional representations of a locally compact group. On the other side there are finite-dimensional representations of a group that is almost a Galois group. The correspondence matches them, and it matches their $L$-factors and $\varepsilon$-factors.

Chapter 1 develops the representation theory of $p$-adic groups that the statement needs. It defines smooth and admissible representations and the Hecke algebra. It defines parabolic induction and Jacquet modules, and it shows that each irreducible representation is a subrepresentation of a representation induced from a supercuspidal one. It treats the group $GL_2$ in detail, it gives the classification of Bernstein and Zelevinsky for $GL_n$ in outline, and it defines Whittaker models. Chapter 2 develops the other side. It recalls the Weil group from Volume 3, defines Weil-Deligne representations, proves the monodromy theorem of Grothendieck, and defines the local factors of a Weil-Deligne representation. It also defines the standard $L$-factor of Godement and Jacquet of a representation of $GL_n(K)$. Chapter 3 states the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_n$, with its characterization by the $L$-factors and $\varepsilon$-factors of pairs. It works out the cases $n = 1$ and $n = 2$, the $L$-packets of $SL_2$, the local Jacquet-Langlands correspondence and the correspondence for $GSp_4$.

The parallel textbooks are the book of Bushnell and Henniart [BH06] on $GL_2$ and the lecture notes of Bernstein on the representations of $p$-adic groups. We follow [BH06] for $GL_2$ and Bernstein's notes for the general theory. The Lean project states the main theorems of Chapter 3. It does not prove them, and each Lean statement is a special form of the theorem. The text says in each case exactly what the Lean statement leaves out.
