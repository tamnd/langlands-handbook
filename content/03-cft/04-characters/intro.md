# Characters and the local Langlands correspondence for GL(1)

Chapter 2 proved that $\mathrm{rec}_K$ is an isomorphism $K^\times \cong W_K^{ab}$. Thus the characters of $K^\times = GL_1(K)$ and the characters of $W_K$ are the same objects. This chapter reads that statement as the first case of the local Langlands correspondence, and it checks that the correspondence respects the invariants that appear in the general case: the conductor, the $L$-factor and the $\varepsilon$-factor.

Section 4.1 studies the characters of $K^\times$ and their conductors. Section 4.2 states the correspondence with its properties and compares the conductor on the two sides. Section 4.3 states the local functional equation of Tate and computes the factors of a character. Section 4.4 treats unramified twists. Section 4.5 extends the correspondence from $GL_1$ to split tori and to the tori $\mathrm{Res}_{L/K} \mathbb{G}_m$, which is the first step toward the dual group.

The notation is that of Chapters 2 and 3. A character of a topological group is a continuous homomorphism to $\mathbb{C}^\times$. The absolute value is normalized by $|\varpi| = q^{-1}$, and $U^{(0)} = \mathcal{O}_K^\times$.
