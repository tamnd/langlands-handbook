# Class field theory and GL(1)

This volume proves the Langlands correspondence for the group $GL_1$. For a local field $K$ the correspondence is a bijection between the characters of $K^\times$ and the characters of the Weil group $W_K$. For a number field it is a bijection between the Hecke characters and the characters of the global Weil group. Both bijections are class field theory, in the form that Weil, Artin and Tate gave it.

The volume starts with the Weil group of a local field, because every later statement of the handbook uses it. Chapter 2 proves local reciprocity. Chapter 3 proves the existence theorem with Lubin-Tate theory. Chapter 4 reads local reciprocity as the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_1$, with conductors, the local factors of Tate and the case of tori. The later chapters do the global theory and Tate's thesis.
