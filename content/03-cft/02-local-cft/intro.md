# Local class field theory

Local class field theory describes the abelian extensions of a nonarchimedean local field $K$ in terms of $K$ itself. The finite abelian extensions of $K$ correspond to the open subgroups of finite index of $K^\times$, and the Galois group of each one is a quotient of $K^\times$. The Weil group of Chapter 1 turns these statements into one isomorphism of topological groups $\mathrm{rec}_K : K^\times \to W_K^{ab}$.

Section 2.1 states the classical theorem and deduces the properties of norm groups. Section 2.2 proves the unramified case in full. Section 2.3 deduces the form of Chapter 1 and states the local Langlands correspondence for $GL_1$. Section 2.4 treats a change of the field, and Section 2.5 computes examples over $\mathbb{Q}_p$.

The chapter does not prove the finite theorem in general. Its standard proof uses Galois cohomology, and the first section outlines it with references. Chapter 3 proves the existence theorem with Lubin-Tate theory and computes $\mathrm{rec}_K$ explicitly. The reader needs Chapter 1 and the basic facts on local fields of Volume 1.
