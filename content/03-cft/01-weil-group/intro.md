# The Weil group of a local field

The absolute Galois group $G_K$ of a local field $K$ is a profinite group, and its abelianization is the profinite completion of $K^\times$. Thus $G_K^{ab}$ is a little too large to be isomorphic to $K^\times$. Weil found the correct group in 1951. It is a dense subgroup $W_K$ of $G_K$ with a finer topology, and its abelianization is isomorphic to $K^\times$ as a topological group.

This chapter defines $W_K$ and proves its first properties. The reader must know Galois theory for infinite extensions and the basic facts on local fields, as in Volume 1. The proof of local reciprocity is in Chapter 2.
