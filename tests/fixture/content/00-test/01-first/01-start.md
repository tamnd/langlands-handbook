## The start

An unramified character of $K^\times$ is fixed by its value on a uniformizer.

**Theorem (unramified).** The reciprocity map sends a uniformizer of $K$ to a geometric Frobenius.

Node: LG0000000A · Lean: `Langlands.localReciprocityGL1_unramified`

*Proof.* Both sides are trivial on the units, and they agree on one uniformizer. $\square$

**Definition.** A character of $K^\times$ is *unramified* when it is trivial on $\mathcal{O}_K^\times$.
